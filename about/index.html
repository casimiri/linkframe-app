About QR TekLid

QR TekLid is a free QR code generator for links, text, Wi-Fi, contact cards, email, phone and SMS. It is built for small businesses, teachers, event organisers and anyone who needs a good-looking code quickly, without creating yet another account.

Codes are generated right in your browser and can be styled with your colours, a frame and your logo. If you need to edit a code after printing it or see how often it is scanned, a free account adds dynamic and smart codes.
