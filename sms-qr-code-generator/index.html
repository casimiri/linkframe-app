SMS QR code generator

An SMS QR code opens the messaging app with your number and a ready-written text. It is a quick way to run keyword opt-ins, votes, contest entries, appointment requests or “text us” support from a printed flyer or screen.

The person scanning sees the message before sending it and can edit it. Standard messaging rates from their carrier apply, just as for any text they send.

How to create it

  1. Enter the phone number that should receive the text.
  2. Write the message that should be pre-filled.
  3. Style the code and add your logo.
  4. Download as PNG or SVG and test it with your phone.

Frequently asked questions

Is it free, and do the codes expire?

Yes, it is free and there is nothing to sign up for. The codes made here are static: the content is stored inside the pattern itself, so they keep working forever and never depend on our servers.

Can I add a logo and my own colours?

Yes. Upload a PNG, JPG, WebP or SVG logo, pick colours or a gradient, and choose dot, corner and frame styles. The logo is embedded in your browser and never leaves your device.

Should I download PNG or SVG for print?

Use SVG for print: it is a vector file that stays sharp at any size, from business cards to posters. PNG (1024 px) is best for websites, documents and social media.

Does it work on both iPhone and Android?

Yes. Both open their built-in messaging app with the number and text filled in.
