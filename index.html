Free QR code generator

QR TekLid turns a link, a Wi-Fi network, a contact card, an email, a phone number or a text message into a QR code in seconds. Anyone who scans it with a phone camera is taken straight to the content, with no app to install.

Everything happens in your browser: type your content, style the code with your colours, a frame and your logo, and download it as PNG or SVG. There is no sign-up, no watermark and no expiry date on the codes you make here.

How to create it

  1. Choose what the code should contain: a link, text, Wi-Fi, a contact and more.
  2. Enter the details. The preview updates as you type.
  3. Optionally pick colours, a frame, dot styles and upload your logo.
  4. Download the code as PNG for screens or SVG for print.

Frequently asked questions

Is it free, and do the codes expire?

Yes, it is free and there is nothing to sign up for. The codes made here are static: the content is stored inside the pattern itself, so they keep working forever and never depend on our servers.

Can I add a logo and my own colours?

Yes. Upload a PNG, JPG, WebP or SVG logo, pick colours or a gradient, and choose dot, corner and frame styles. The logo is embedded in your browser and never leaves your device.

Should I download PNG or SVG for print?

Use SVG for print: it is a vector file that stays sharp at any size, from business cards to posters. PNG (1024 px) is best for websites, documents and social media.

Can I change where a code points after printing it?

Not with a static code. For that you need a dynamic code, which you can create with a free account (up to 2 saved codes) and edit at any time, with scan statistics included.
