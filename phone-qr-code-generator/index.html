Phone number QR code generator

A phone QR code lets people call you without typing your number. When scanned, the phone opens the dialer with your number ready, and one tap starts the call. It works well on vehicles, shop signs, business cards, rental listings and service stickers.

Enter the number in international format, starting with + and the country code, so the call connects wherever the person scanning happens to be.

How to create it

  1. Enter the phone number with its country code, for example +1 555 123 4567.
  2. Pick colours and a frame, and add your logo if you like.
  3. Scan the preview to check the right number appears.
  4. Download as PNG or SVG.

Frequently asked questions

Is it free, and do the codes expire?

Yes, it is free and there is nothing to sign up for. The codes made here are static: the content is stored inside the pattern itself, so they keep working forever and never depend on our servers.

Can I add a logo and my own colours?

Yes. Upload a PNG, JPG, WebP or SVG logo, pick colours or a gradient, and choose dot, corner and frame styles. The logo is embedded in your browser and never leaves your device.

Should I download PNG or SVG for print?

Use SVG for print: it is a vector file that stays sharp at any size, from business cards to posters. PNG (1024 px) is best for websites, documents and social media.

Does the phone call the number straight away?

No. The dialer opens with the number filled in and the person decides whether to place the call.
