Privacy policy

QR TekLid is operated by the QR TekLid team, reachable at qr@teklid.net. This policy explains what data we process and why.

QR codes made without an account are generated in your browser and are not stored by us. Logos you upload there are embedded on your device and never leave it.

If you create an account, we store your email address, your display name, the codes you save and their logos. To provide scan statistics for your dynamic codes, each scan records the device type, operating system, browser, user agent, referrer, country, city and a salted hash of the scanner’s IP address (pseudonymised, never stored in clear). We do not sell this data. We process it to provide the service you signed up for and delete it when you delete your account.

The service relies on these providers, which receive your IP address when you use it: Supabase (database, sign-in, file storage and the app’s scripts and styles), GitHub Pages (hosting of these pages) and Google Fonts (the typeface).

Our public pages show ads from Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to serve and measure ads. Visitors in the EU and UK are asked for consent through Google’s consent message before personalised ads are shown.

How Google uses information from sites that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, or object to its processing, by writing to qr@teklid.net. You also have the right to complain to your data protection authority.
