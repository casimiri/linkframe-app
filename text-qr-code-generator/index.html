Text QR code generator

A text QR code stores a message directly in the pattern. When scanned, the phone simply shows the text, with no internet connection needed. It is handy for instructions, serial numbers, short notes, riddles for a treasure hunt or labels on equipment.

Because the text lives inside the code, more words make a denser pattern. Keep it to a few sentences for reliable scanning, and print the code larger if you need to store a longer message.

How to create it

  1. Type or paste your message.
  2. Style the code with colours, a frame or your logo.
  3. Scan the preview to check the text shows correctly.
  4. Download as PNG or SVG.

Frequently asked questions

Is it free, and do the codes expire?

Yes, it is free and there is nothing to sign up for. The codes made here are static: the content is stored inside the pattern itself, so they keep working forever and never depend on our servers.

Can I add a logo and my own colours?

Yes. Upload a PNG, JPG, WebP or SVG logo, pick colours or a gradient, and choose dot, corner and frame styles. The logo is embedded in your browser and never leaves your device.

Should I download PNG or SVG for print?

Use SVG for print: it is a vector file that stays sharp at any size, from business cards to posters. PNG (1024 px) is best for websites, documents and social media.

How much text can a QR code hold?

Technically a few thousand characters, but long texts create very dense codes that are hard to scan. For best results keep it under about 300 characters.
